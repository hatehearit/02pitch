01What is a cookie

A cookie is a small text file placed in your browser by a website. Some are necessary for the site to work. Others help us understand how the site is used. We try to use as few as possible.

02What we use

Necessary (always on)

These cookies are required for the site to function. They cannot be turned off.

Analytics (off by default)

These cookies help us understand which pages are read and where readers drop off. Anonymised where possible. We do not use them to identify you, and we do not share the data with third parties.

Marketing (off by default, currently unused)
