How much does this cost?

Starts at $390 for a narrative-only pass on an existing deck. Full narrative plus visual build runs higher, depends on scope. You’ll get a real number before we start, not after.

How long does it take?

Narrative pass: 24–48 hours. Full build: about a week, depending on how much of your story is already figured out versus needs rework.

I already have a deck. Do you rebuild it from scratch?

Depends what’s broken. Sometimes the structure’s fine and it just needs tighter argument and better slide order. Sometimes it needs a full rebuild. We tell you which before you pay for either.

I don’t have a deck yet, just notes and a rough pitch. Can you still help?

Yes. Earlier is actually easier, less to unwind.

Why should I trust you with this over a design agency?

Because we’re not a design agency. The person running this has raised money and pitched investors directly, not just designed slides for people who have. The story comes first, the visuals come after.

Who actually does the work?

Two people. One handles the narrative and pitch logic, one handles the design execution. Not a template farm, not outsourced.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, if you want one before sharing anything.

What if I don’t like the direction?

One revision round is included at every tier. If the core argument’s off, we fix that first, that’s the part that actually matters to investors.

Do you only work with tech startups?

No. Doesn’t matter what you’re building, if you’re raising and need the argument tightened, that’s the job.

What do you need from me to start?

Whatever you’ve got. Existing deck, a Notion doc, bullet points, a voice memo. We’ll ask what’s missing.
