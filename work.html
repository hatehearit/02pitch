Legal-tech · Pre-seed · $500K

ClauseIQ: AI contract review for real estate lawyers.

The original deck led with a market-size slide and a feature list. We moved the founder’s own practice experience to slide one, made the AI a tool inside a real workflow, not the headline, and cut the TAM to focus on a $60M beachhead.

Review time dropped from 40 to 7 minutes for early users. The story stopped reading like a product tour and started reading like a founder who had lived the problem.

Fintech · Pre-seed · $600K

FinFlow: cash flow forecasting for small businesses.

Strong tech, weak hook. We led with the 82% failure stat and made the founder’s prior SMB lending experience the credibility anchor.

Three-tier pricing laid out plain, traction framed as “cash we already recovered for them,” not as a vanity chart. 25 customers, $4.2K MRR at handoff.

Health-tech · Pre-seed · $500K

NutriAI: personalized nutrition for the modern workforce.

Corporate wellness is a graveyard of generic apps. We reframed the wedge around “low engagement, low ROI” on the employer side, made personalization the moat, and pushed the per-employee pricing as a line item HR can sign off without a board meeting.

Targeting 10 enterprise pilots post-round.
