About · Two people

Two people. One deck,
end to end.

Founder-led. Built and sold a business, pitched investors directly, not just designed slides for people who did.

One of us handles the narrative and pitch logic. The other handles design execution. No team, no agency, no template farm. If you email us, one of the two will write back the same day.

We’re not a design agency with opinions about storytelling. We’re storytellers who know what an investor actually reads, because we’ve sat where you sit and watched the deck land or not.

Process

How the work moves.

  1. 01
    Diagnose. We read your deck or notes and tell you, in plain language, what’s a story problem and what’s a design problem. We charge for the diagnostic, but if the deck doesn’t need a rebuild, we tell you that.
  2. 02
    Rebuild the argument. Narrative structure first, slide by slide. The why now, the wedge, the unfair insight, the ask. We sequence it so the investor gets it on the second slide, not the tenth.
  3. 03
    Execute. Visual build. Clean, restrained, no agency nonsense. The slides exist to support the argument, not the other way around.
  4. 04
    Deliver. Final deck plus one revision round. You walk into the meeting with the narrative tight, the slides out of the way, and a clear answer to “so what.”
