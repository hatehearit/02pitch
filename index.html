The problem

Most decks fail because they’re a slide-design exercise,
not an argument.

Investors skim in minutes. If the why now, why you, why this doesn’t jump out fast, the deck fails regardless of how it looks.

Pretty slides with a weak narrative get the same result as ugly slides with a weak narrative. They both get ignored.

How it works

Four steps. The first one is honest.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    We read your current deck or notes and tell you, in plain language, what’s a story problem and what’s a design problem. Sometimes the answer is neither. Most of the time it’s story.

  2. 02

    Rebuild the argument

    Narrative structure first, slide by slide. The why now, the wedge, the unfair insight, the ask. We sequence it so the investor gets it on the second slide, not the tenth.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Visual build. Clean, restrained, no agency nonsense. The slides exist to support the argument, not the other way around.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Final deck plus one revision round. You walk into the meeting with the narrative tight, the slides out of the way, and a clear answer to “so what.”
