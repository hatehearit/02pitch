01What data we collect

We collect the minimum data needed to provide our services:

  • Information you give us — name, email, company name, fundraising stage, and any message you include when you contact us through the form or by email.
  • Files you send us — pitch decks, notes, voice memos, or other materials you choose to share for the purpose of the engagement. We treat these as confidential.
  • Technical data — anonymised usage data (pages visited, time on page, device type, approximate country) collected only after you accept analytics cookies.

02Why we collect it

We use the data to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide the free deck read.
  • Deliver the services you have paid for.
  • Improve the site: which pages are useful, which are not, where readers drop off.
  • Comply with legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your data, ever. We do not use it for advertising.

03Cookies

We use a small number of cookies. The full list lives in our Cookie Policy. In short:

  • Necessary cookies — remember your cookie preferences. Cannot be turned off.
  • Analytics cookies — anonymised page stats. Off by default.
  • Marketing cookies — currently unused. Off by default.

You can change your preferences at any time using the link in the footer.

04Who we share data with

We share your data only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the site: hosting, email delivery, payment. Each is bound by a data processing agreement.
  • Authorities when required by law (tax filings, lawful requests).

We do not share with advertisers, brokers, or third parties for their own purposes.

05How long we keep it

  • Enquiry data (name, email, message) — up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Engagement files (decks, drafts, invoices) — 7 years for tax and accounting, then deleted.
  • Analytics data — up to 14 months, then aggregated or deleted.

06Your rights

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what data we hold on you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data, where we are not legally required to keep it.
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent.
  • Export your data in a portable format.

Write to hello@deckstudios.co. We respond within 30 days.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or California, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
